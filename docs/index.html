7. How the work was done, in one paragraph

Eight regional teams first collected sources on robot and AI governance (round 1). AI agents and scripts then searched official legal databases, standards catalogues and treaty archives for the instruments that round had missed (round 2), and a third round recovered the texts that databases had blocked, with an honestly identified crawler that does not imitate a browser. Every document was read by an AI model against the same 39 questions; a second model and re-runs measured consistency; six adversarial reviewers read each version; independent AI auditors re-read random samples of the binding set. No person has labelled a document, so every number is a well-measured estimate with an honest error bar, and the next step in the study is a person, not a model.
