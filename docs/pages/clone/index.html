Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 on machinery

Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2023 on machinery and repealing Directive 2006/42/EC and Council Directive 73/361/EEC

▤ Legislation⚖ Legally bindingEuropean Union · EUin force

EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 replaces the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC. It sets essential health and safety requirements for machinery, related products and partly completed machinery. It extends coverage to digital safety risks, including self-evolving behaviour, autonomy and cybersecurity. It requires third-party conformity assessment for machine-learning safety systems and sets out economic operators’ obligations and market surveillance.

What the document contains

What’s coveredEssential health and safety requirements for machinery; software as a safety component; conformity assessment including third-party assessment for self-evolving safety systems; cybersecurity of safety functions; obligations of economic operators; market surveillance and penalties.
What’s missingNo provisions on liability for damage (left to the Product Liability Directive), no robot-specific definitions, and no rules on who may operate a machine once sold.
Key takeaways
  • Replaces Directive 2006/42/EC with a directly applicable regulation covering machinery, related products and partly completed machinery.
  • Safety components include software; systems with self-evolving behaviour ensuring safety functions require third-party conformity assessment (Annex I).
  • Risk assessment must cover evolving behaviour and varying levels of autonomy, plus interactions in coordinated machinery.
  • Manufacturers must protect against malicious third-party corruption affecting safety and make source code available to authorities on request.
  • Substantial modification by physical or digital means triggers a new conformity assessment; Member States must set penalties.
