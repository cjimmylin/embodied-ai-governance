Rules follow machines into public space. Usually after the first accident.

Drones

Rules mostly 2016 to 2021, after the near-misses at airports

Self-driving cars

Rules from 2017, after the test fleets, and the first deaths

Sidewalk robots

State laws 2018 to 2022, after the robots were already on the pavement

Three quarters of the 278 rules date from 2019 or later; 105 of them from 2024 to 2026.
