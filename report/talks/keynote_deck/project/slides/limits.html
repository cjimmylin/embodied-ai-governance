What we do not know

The honest edges of the map

74 official sources refused our crawler, mostly France, Norway, Korea, Taiwan and Chinese provinces. They are listed for a person with a browser. Our crawler identifies itself honestly and does not pretend to be one.

Old statutes hide their robot provisions. A road-traffic act from 1960 can carry a 2023 article on automated driving; we could date the provision in only 28 of the 278 rules. Trend lines on binding law are therefore floors, not histories.

English is 74% of the collection. Chinese, Japanese, Spanish and Portuguese texts were read in the original, but the search was richest in English and in the EU.

No human has labelled a document. The next step in this study is a person, not a model.
