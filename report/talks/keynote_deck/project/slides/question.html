The question

Software AI makes decisions. Embodied AI makes decisions and then moves.

A chatbot that goes wrong produces a bad sentence. A robot, a drone or a self-driving car that goes wrong produces a bad event: a collision, a crushed hand, a fall. The questions change the moment the AI has a body.

Can it hurt someone?

How close may it work to people?

Who pays when it breaks something?

How much may it decide alone?

So we asked a plain question: anywhere in the world, what rules exist for AI with a body, and what do they actually say?
