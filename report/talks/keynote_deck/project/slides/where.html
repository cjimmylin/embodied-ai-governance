The United States (75 rules) legislates state by state: delivery robots, autonomous vehicles, drones over prisons.

The European Union (40) regulates machines as products: the Machinery Regulation, drone regulations, the AI Act reaching safety components.

Japan, China and Korea (57 together) are the only places with laws that say 'robot' in the title: Korea's Intelligent Robots Act, Japan's Level-4 traffic rules, China's city regulations for driverless cars, and the 2025 to 2026 plans for 'embodied intelligence' in Beijing and Shanghai.
