What it means

For governance: three moves before the next machines arrive

Write the rules for the robots we will live with, before the incidents

Companions, carers and humanoids have zero, zero and four binding rules. Drones got theirs after near-misses at airports. The pattern is avoidable.

Answer the control question, do not just raise it

Who may let the machine decide what, under which conditions, checked by whom. Aviation law shows it can be done; the other sectors mostly gesture at it.

Make the safety knowledge public

When a law points to a standard, the standard becomes law in effect. Fifty-seven of our sixty-one standards are behind a paywall. Rules people cannot read are rules people cannot follow.
