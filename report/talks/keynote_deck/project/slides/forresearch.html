What it means

For AI research: we are studying robots that nobody is regulating

1

The research agenda and the legal agenda are inverted. Four papers in ten on robot governance are about companion robots; three rules in a hundred are. Our evidence on human-robot interaction, deception, attachment and dependency has nowhere in law to land.

2

'Autonomy' is a legal word now, and it is underspecified. 171 rules invoke it, 73 define it. Researchers who can translate levels of autonomy into testable conditions are writing the next decade of law whether they mean to or not.

3

Reading law at scale is now possible, and it needs the same rigour as any instrument. Blind reading, inter-reader agreement, independent audits, counting units stated in advance. Governance science can be measured, not only argued.
