To remember

Five things to take home

1

Robots are governed sideways. 278 binding rules reach them; 25 name AI. They are aviation, road, machinery and product law.

2

The robots we will live with have almost none. Companions 0, humanoids 0, care robots 4, among 429 documents about them.

3

The body changes the rulebook. Physical safety, certification, accountability and liability lead; transparency, fairness and dignity barely appear.

4

Mention is not detail. Six rules in ten raise the control question; fewer than three in ten answer it. Duties fall on operators, rarely on the people who write the software.

5

Every count depends on where you looked. Ours went 5, 299, 195, 278. Ask any number about AI regulation how it was made.
