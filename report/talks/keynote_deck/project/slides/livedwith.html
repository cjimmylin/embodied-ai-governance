Across the collection, 429 documents discuss these three kinds of robot. Four of them are binding rules, and those four are general laws that mention care robots in passing: a Salvadoran robotics law, a Korean robot-industry act, two Japanese special-zone ordinances. The one text written specifically for care robots is a voluntary standard, ISO 13482, from 2014.
