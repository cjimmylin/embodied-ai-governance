Headline one

Robots are governed sideways

278

binding rules in the world that reach robots: statutes, regulations and decrees with legal force, counted once each

25

of them name artificial intelligence at all

201

contain any provision on autonomy or automation

The rest are aviation law, road-traffic law, machinery law, product-safety law and medical-device law. They reach robots because a drone is an aircraft, a self-driving car is a vehicle and a robot arm is a machine. Almost none of them were written with 'AI' in mind.
