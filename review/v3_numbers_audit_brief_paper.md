# v3 numbers audit: brief.md and paper.md

Audited: `report/src/brief.md` and `report/src/paper.md` (References section skipped). Every sentence containing a number or percentage was checked against, in order of authority, `data/master_table.csv` (recomputed with pandas using the set definitions in `scripts/analysis_common.py::load()`; instruments = doc_class in binding/soft/draft; robot-specific = instrument and relevance >= 2), `report/results_digest.md`, `data/results.json`, `tables/*.csv`, and for v2 comparisons `../embodied-ai-governance-v2/report/results_digest.md` and `../embodied-ai-governance-v2/tables/`. Figure data files (`figures/data/F06_cumulative_instruments.csv`, `F33_term_trends.csv`) and the round-2 annotation files (`annotations/round2/*.json`) were used where the text cites a figure or an annotation cost.

**Summary.** brief.md: 73 quantitative claims checked: 65 MATCH, 6 MINOR, 2 MISMATCH. paper.md: 110 quantitative claims checked: 88 MATCH, 12 MINOR, 10 MISMATCH. The two mismatches in the brief recur in the paper (abstract, Section 3, Section 5, Section 6), so the distinct problems are nine; the three that matter most are listed at the end.

## Notes on the "689" and coverage-audit problems

- Three different counts of robot-specific instruments are in circulation and all three are produced by the pipeline: 689 (`21_round2_report.py`: instrument and relevance >= 2, no AI-scope filter; used in T53, the abstract, Section 3 and Section 5), 681 (same filter restricted to the embodied set, which is what the nesting sentences in the brief and Section 3 assert), and 677 (`18_analysis_D.py`, Table 33: additionally requires `is_governance_document`). The eight documents that separate 689 from 681 are EU machinery directives 89/392/EEC and 98/37/EC, the UK Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 1992/2008/2026, ISO 13849-1, ISO/TR 22100-4 and IEC 62061, all annotated `relevance = 2` but `ai_scope = not_ai`. The "119" general-AI instruments with robot provisions appears nowhere in the data; the pipeline value is 127.
- The coverage audit (T25, `captured_as_document`) is a title-regex match. It misses both US executive orders although they are in the collection (D0915, D0918), so "29 of 38" and "10 in v2" are floors; the actual figures are at least 31 and 12. Section 5.1 of the paper already lists Executive Order 14110 as being in the set, which contradicts the audit paragraph two paragraphs later.
