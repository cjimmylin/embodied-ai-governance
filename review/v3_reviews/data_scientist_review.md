## Verdict

The arithmetic of v3 reproduces (every headline count I recomputed matches: 3,148 / 1,589 / 982 / 808 / 681 / 127 / 299 / 268 / 265 / 172 / 100 / 116 / 78 / 58 / 45 / 40 / 60 / 737 / 476 / 1,345 / 2 of 191 and 31 of 260), the v2 fixes still hold (round-1 labels are byte-identical between the v2 and v3 master tables for all 1,654 shared documents; the AI Act, EO 14110, Taiwan stub, bills-as-binding and primary-category rules are all still applied), and the numbers audits did their job. The problem is one level up. Round 2 added 1,637 documents that are instruments only, harvested from legal databases rather than found by topical search, half the length of round-1 texts, 24% catalogue or landing pages, annotated on scope pages for standards, and never checked by a second model, a re-run or a full-text read. That design enters every "v2 finding reversed" claim as a confound that the genre adjustment cannot remove, because within the instrument genre the embodied side is now 92% round-2 and the general side 30%. When I add collection round, log word count and capture tier to the published adjusted model, or restrict to round-1 documents, the three headline reversals (the ethical core is thinner in robot rules even within genre; regions now differ on values; proximity and liability are declining) shrink to a few robust items or vanish, while the v2 trends the paper says "do not survive" (cybersecurity, risk tiering, conformity assessment) are still present in the v2 population and are merely diluted. Separately, the instrument counts that carry the new lead finding rest on a unit of analysis that counts every yearly CASA renewal, EEA Joint Committee decision, language-version corrigendum, commencement order, state-code section and single-article excerpt as one "binding instrument reaching robots", and the "technical standards specify physical safety in depth in 47%" statistic is computed on a population that is 74% catalogue pages, which the report's own definitions say must be excluded from statements about what instruments say. The study needs a major revision of its comparative claims and its instrument unit before the v3 findings can replace the v2 ones; the descriptive inventory and the route finding (binding law reaches robots through sector law) are sound.

## The five changes that would most improve validity

1. **Model the collection, not just the genre.** Put collection round, log word count and capture tier into every adjusted model (T20, T28, T17), report the embodied-vs-general contrast within binding instruments and within substantive captures as the primary strata, and give round-1-only estimates wherever a v2 result is said to have changed. On my re-fits this turns "eleven of fourteen core principles lower" into about six, removes the proximity/liability decline, and restores the v2 cybersecurity, risk-tiering and conformity trends within the discourse documents. The abstract, Finding 3, Finding 5 and the brief's paragraphs 5, 7 and 8 should be rewritten from those estimates.
2. **Fix the unit of analysis.** Define an instrument family and report every instrument count (Finding 1, T53 to T55, T59, S01, the brief's 299/265/172/100, the top-jurisdiction list, the Oceania and EU regional statements, the drone and vehicle gap indices) at family level beside document level, after removing corrigenda, commencement and levy-collection acts, event and named-person approvals, status pages, tables of contents and single-article excerpts from the instrument tier. State how much each headline moves.
3. **Restore the capture-tier rule everywhere.** Rebuild T09 (and any statistic about what standards or binding instruments "specify in depth") on content-analysed captures, impose a minimum-text rule for level-2 labels, and print the landing-page share next to every standards number. Finding 4 and the brief's "standards specify physical safety in depth in 47%" currently describe 177 catalogue pages.
4. **Validate the round-2 labels on the fields that carry the findings.** A stratified round-2 reliability sample (second model and re-run; binding, scope-page standards, non-English, short texts) for principles, mechanisms, topics and relevance, with kappa reported by text length, before any v3 result is presented as replacing a v2 one.
5. **Separate enactment year from version year** for harvested consolidated texts, and either exclude consolidated texts from the time series or date them by the amendment that introduced the relevant provisions; drop the sector-law "first appearance" years and state how many 2026 documents are consolidation versions.
