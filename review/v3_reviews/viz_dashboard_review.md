# Adversarial review of the v3 figures and dashboard

Reviewer role: data-visualisation and dashboard QA. Scope: the 44 light figures F01-F52 (`figures/F*.png`), their 44 dark variants, the CSV twins in `figures/data/`, the `CAPTIONS` dict in `scripts/16_build_reports.py`, the figure code in `scripts/20_figures_v2.py`, `18_analysis_D.py`, `19_fulltext_validation.py`, `21_round2_report.py` and `figstyle.py`, and the dashboard (`dashboard/index.html`, `artifact.html`, generated by `scripts/10_dashboard.py`). Standard: the dataviz skill (`anti-patterns.md`, `marks-and-anatomy.md`, `color-formula.md`, `palette.md`, `validate_palette.js`). Context: the v1 review (`review/viz_dashboard_review.md`) and the v2 change log (`review/adversarial_review_and_changes.md`).

Method: every light PNG read at full size; every dark PNG read on 2x2 contact sheets (`shots/f_dark_sheet00-10.png`); every computed title re-derived from its CSV twin; the plot-area share of each image measured from the SVG (axes patch extent / viewBox width); the palette re-run through `validate_palette.js` in both modes; the figures viewed as embedded in `report/analysis_report.html` at 1000 px (`shots/r01_report_embed_F*.png`); the dashboard tested with headless Chromium (Playwright) at 1360x900 light and dark and 400x860 (mobile emulation, light and dark), with tile numbers recomputed from `data/master_table.csv` through `analysis_common.load()`. `scripts/shot.js` was re-run (its three outputs are `shots/d00_regenerated_*.png`); 27 further screenshots are in `shots/`. No project file was modified.

## 1. Verdict in ten lines

1. The v2 fixes held where they were mechanical: dark heatmaps are now monotone (reversed `SEQ` on the dark surface), zero cells are visible, small-n columns are hatched, F23 is redrawn as small multiples, F34/F37 legends are outside, slug labels are gone for topics, mechanisms, sectors and issuers, the dashboard is a real HTML document with a viewport, keyboard access, dialog semantics, CSV export and a phone layout. All 44 figures now have a CSV twin.
2. The headline new problem is that the computed finding-titles do not fit the figure. `bbox_inches="tight"` grows the canvas to the title, so in 36 of 44 figures the plot occupies only 31-56 % of the image width (F22 31 %, F34 33 %, F11 34 %, F30 34 %, F14 35 %). Embedded at the report's 900 px reading width, F22's heatmap is 290 px wide with 4 px cell text (`shots/r01_report_embed_F22.png`). This is the single fix that would most improve the package.
3. Four figures say something their own data contradict: F40's hard-coded title ("the one category where research outruns governance; vehicles, arms and drones are the reverse") while ten categories carry the CI-excludes-zero star and "arms" does not; F23's second clause ("physical safety ... specified in depth least often") when physical safety has the highest in-depth share of the six; F39's subtitle ("core principles above the line") when a double `invert_yaxis()` on a shared axis puts them below; F28's shares (70/20/11 = 101 %) because the numerator includes the "to 2014" period the denominator excludes.
4. F31 is still unreadable: the dendrogram leaf labels overprint the heatmap row labels ("North AmericaOceania", "InternationalNorth America") and the dendrogram's leaf order is the reverse of the matrix's row order, so the two panels do not line up.
5. The title generator leaves grammar and case artefacts: "drones uas" (F13, from `.capitalize()`), "p: Non-maleficence" (F35, from `lower1`), "proposal or bills" (F22), "Autonomous road vehicles peaks in North America; Drones UAS peaks in Oceania; ..." (F12), lists joined without "and" (F42).
6. Hue discipline is only partly kept: embodied = blue / general = orange holds in F02-F03, F07, F18-F20, F24, F29, F39, F51/52, but blue/orange also mean English/Spanish (F10), binding/drafts (F06), embodied-specific/core (F38, where orange is the embodied side), captured/not (F41), principle/robot type (F36), v2/v3 (F51), and yellow stands in for "general" in F52.
7. Several n's disagree across figures that share a population: embodied instruments are 804 (F18 legend, F24, F39), 808 (F51, round-2 tables) and 812 (the denominator behind F18's percentages, F04's governance n is 810); the round-2 funnel says 1,716 annotated (F50) but F52's class bars sum to 1,637.
8. The dashboard's numbers are right (every tile and start-button count reproduces from `master_table.csv`; zero console errors; CSV export rows match the filter), but its methods panel is v1 text: "Chinese-language documents are essentially absent" (v3 has 266, F10 shows 406 CJK), "Europe is 44 % ... English 78 %" (42 % / 75 %), "a 10 % sample" (160 of 3,291 = 4.9 %), kappa "0.89 / 0.95 / 0.84" (data: 0.85 / 0.91 / 0.88).
9. Two dashboard interactions mislead: the "Technical standards" start button is a substring search for "ISO" among soft-law documents (230 hits, of which 167 are standards; 115 of the 282 usable technical standards are missed, 63 strategies and guidelines are included because "comparison", "supervision" and "advisory" contain "iso"); and the "Columns" buttons silently rewrite the filters, so Rules -> Research gives "0 match".
10. Of the three user questions, "binding drone rules in Europe" is answered in three clicks (57 rows, pandas 56 plus one title match); "which countries have humanoid instruments" needs a table scan of 27 rows (no country chart); "what changed with round 2" cannot be answered from the dashboard (no before/after, no round-2 numbers in the story or methods text, and round-2 documents are labelled "general AI" in the drawer).

## 2. Systemic findings (referenced as S1-S10 in the table)

- **S1 Title overflow expands the canvas (High).** `title()` writes the computed finding plus a subtitle as one two-line string; nothing wraps it. With `bbox_inches="tight"` the saved image grows to the title width while the axes keep their `figsize`. Measured plot-area share of image width: F01 42, F02 63, F03 39, F04 39, F05 51, F06 41, F07 61, F08 46, F10 54, F11 34, F12 39, F13 38, F14 35, F16 43, F17 38, F18 47, F19 41, F20 45, F21 43, F22 31, F23 82, F24 43, F25 37, F26 42, F27 54, F28 53, F29 53, F30 34, F31 48, F33 50, F34 33, F35 46, F36 41, F37 56, F38 44, F39 75, F40 72, F41 63, F42 56, F43 65, F44 61, F50 51, F51 66, F52 61. Only F23, F39, F40 (static or wrapped titles) are above 70 %. The report CSS (`img{max-width:100%}`) then renders every figure at 900 px, so a 3,661 px wide F22 shows its heatmap at ~290 px (`shots/r01_report_embed_F22.png`, `_F11.png`, `_F34.png`). Fix: wrap the title to the axes width (`textwrap.fill(main, 95)` at 9 pt for 7.2 in; subtitle at 8 pt), or shorten the computed sentence to one clause, and assert in `fs.save()` that the saved width is at most 1.15x `figsize[0]*dpi`.
- **S2 Computed and static titles that misstate the data (High).** F40, F23, F39, F28 as in the verdict; F43's static "weakest fields are date basis, page quality and mechanisms" when the three lowest full-text kappas are date basis (0.71), stance (0.72) and mechanisms (0.73), page quality is fifth; F12's "peaks in" is computed over all regions including the hatched one. The static titles in `18_analysis_D.py` and `19_fulltext_validation.py` were written for v2 data and never recomputed.
- **S3 Title grammar and case (Medium).** `.capitalize()` lower-cases the rest of the string (F13 "drones uas"); `lower1()` lower-cases "P:" (F35); `DCS[c].lower()+"s"` gives "proposal or bills" (F22) and "voluntary guidance / standards" (F13, F22); F12 strings four "X peaks in Y" clauses with capitalised category names mid-sentence; F42 joins with ", " instead of `joinl`. Robot-category names are still `nice()` slugs ("Drones UAS", "Humanoid general purpose", "Rehabilitation exoskeleton prosthetic", "Toys entertainment robots", "Biohybrid or cyborg") in every figure and in the dashboard.
- **S4 Hue reassignment (Medium).** See verdict line 6. The brief's rule "embodied = blue, general = orange throughout" is broken in F06, F10, F36, F38, F41, F51, F52. F38 is the worst case because it is the paper's headline model figure and there orange means the embodied-specific principles.
- **S5 Legend and label collisions that survived or appeared (Medium).** F04: the two legends share one row and clip each other ("Under developmen", "Superseded / repea"); the in-segment label "13% (50" is cut at the axis edge and "15% (55)" overflows its 15 % segment. F28: the legend sits on the 2025-2026 "no position" bar (79 %). F31: as in the verdict. F39, F43, F44: the legend is drawn inside the axes on the bottom data rows, with legend markers abutting data points (F39 Transparency/Justice rows, F43 stance/date-basis rows). F41: six instrument names truncated at 60 characters ("Germany Autonomous Driving Act (StVG amendment, Level 4) 202"). F26: white "45%", "46%", "17%" on faded (alpha 0.45) fills, 1.8:1.
- **S6 Dark variants are layout-identical but not colour-selected for ordinal ramps (Medium).** `heatmap()` re-steps the sequential ramp for the dark surface (correct), but the hand-built ordinal ramps do not: F04's lifecycle steps `SEQ[9], SEQ[11]` (#1c5cab, #104281) sit at 2.6:1 and 1.8:1 on #1a1a19, so "Under development" and "Published, current" nearly vanish; F27's "Calls for new regulation" (#1c5cab) is 2.6:1. F23's dark variant keeps the x-label key "light: mentioned; dark: treated in depth" although on the dark surface the alpha-0.35 "mentioned" bar is the darker one. Everything else in dark mode (ramps, hatching, in-cell ink, lines, markers) is equivalent to light (`shots/f_dark_sheet*.png`).
- **S7 Inconsistent n for the same population (Medium).** Embodied governance instruments: 804 (`emb.gov & doc_class in 3 instrument classes`, F18/F24/F39 legends, F40's bootstrap), 808 (`doc_class in 3 classes & embodied_set`, F51, T53), 812 (`gov & embodied_set`, the denominator of F18's CSV percentages: 13/1.6 % = 812, and the dashboard's "812 robot rules"). F04 prints n=810 for the same group. Round 2: F50 says 1,716 content-analysed, F52's bars sum to 1,637 (79 documents merged as duplicates in assembly are not a funnel step). F08 labels `gov & embodied_set` counts "robot-specific instruments" while the paper reserves that term for relevance >= 2 (681 documents).
- **S8 n and small-n flags missing where the figure is a percentage (Low-Medium).** No n on F34 (two periods), F38 (2,485), F40, F41 (3,148), F43/F44 (160); F27 shows n=28 and n=20 rows at full weight although the stated threshold is 30; F31 includes Africa (n=26) unflagged and prints no n.
- **S9 Legend rule (Low).** F06 (four lines) and F23 (two series) have no legend box; F06 relies on end labels with leader lines (acceptable), F23 on the x-axis label. F05's legend carries a "fewer than 30 documents" entry whose hollow marker is invisible on the surface and which matches no point.
- **S10 Caption-figure drift (Low).** `CAPTIONS["F02"]` says 2010-2026, the plot is 2014-2026; `CAPTIONS["F39"]` says nothing about the line that the figure's own subtitle mislabels; F26's subtitle says "regions with fewer than 20 embodied documents omitted" when none is omitted; F30 keeps "Asimov's Laws" as a row while F29 omits it with a footnote.

Palette: `figstyle.CAT_LIGHT/CAT_DARK/SEQ` are still hex-for-hex the reference palette; `validate_palette.js` passes both modes (light WARN as expected for aqua/yellow/magenta below 3:1, relieved by in-segment labels and CSV twins). No dual axes, rainbows or dashed grids anywhere.
