# Adversarial review of v3: LLM annotation validity, measurement and reproducibility

← Back to the **[project hub](https://github.com/cjimmylin/embodied-ai-governance/blob/main/docs/index.html)** (all deliverables) · [live hub page on claude.ai](https://claude.ai/artifact/QRA3FAoGPUDJeNAg4QUQoA)

Reviewer role: AI researcher (LLM-based annotation, measurement validity, reproducibility). Object: version 3 of "Governing machines that act in the world" (package `/home/user/embodied-ai-governance-v3/`), with particular attention to the 1,637 round-2 documents that entered the collection annotated once, metadata-blind, under the v2 extended-excerpt policy. Everything below was recomputed read-only from `data/master_table.csv`, `annotations/round2/*.json`, the stored model inputs and raw texts in the round-2 workspace (`/home/user/collection-round-2/annotations/round2/_inputs/`, `/home/user/collection-round-2/texts/`), `data/round2_inventory.csv`, the scripts and the reports. Items fixed in v2 (leakage of the collector cover block, v1 excerpt length, lexicon bugs, reliability against the wrong run) are not re-raised except where the fix regressed or did not reach round 2. No project file was edited; the only file written is this review.

## 1. Verdict

The v3 annotation pipeline is better documented than most LLM-annotation studies (fixed codebook, schema-enforced output, stored inputs, cost and prompt hash per document, five round-1 consistency checks, a frank limitations section), and the round-2 collection is a genuine improvement in instrument coverage. But the round-2 layer, which now supplies 92 per cent of the embodied governance instruments and all of the Chinese and Japanese material, rests on labels that are unvalidated in a way the paper does not disclose and in two places cannot have been produced from the text. First, the v2 "repaired" cleaning rule deletes most Chinese text before the model sees it: a median of 24 per cent of each Chinese document reached the annotator, 97 Chinese documents whose raw text names robots, AI, drones or automated driving had none of those terms in the model input, and 83 of those were labelled "not AI"; the paper nevertheless presents Chinese as the collection's second language and uses the "not AI" labels to exclude 577 documents. Second, for the longest statutes the operative articles lie entirely outside the 110,000-character window and the model filled the fields from what it knows about the instrument (the Japanese Industrial Safety and Health Ordinance is coded for industrial robots with zero robot mentions in its input), which the codebook forbids and no check can detect. Third, there is no reliability evidence for round 2 in v3; a stratified round-2 sample was planned and partly run after the package was frozen, and on the 59 and 81 documents completed the instability sits exactly on the fields that define the analysis sets (ai_scope re-run kappa 0.83, instrument status vs Opus 0.68, set membership flips for 7 per cent of documents). Fourth, the one validity check offered for round 2, agreement with the "collectors' catalogue metadata", compares the model with Claude subagents and with per-source constants written into the harvest scripts; it is not independent. Fifth, 217 landing and catalogue pages and 81 texts under 1,000 characters are counted as governance instruments in round 2, repeating at twenty times the scale the catalogue-page problem v2 had fixed by hand for round 1, and the headline reversal of the core-principle finding ("eleven of fourteen lower among embodied instruments") does not appear within round-1 instruments and tracks text length and capture quality, which the adjusted models do not include. My own reading of 29 round-2 inputs against their labels found twelve with an error in a set-defining field (class, binding nature, AI scope or relevance) and seven more where depth scores of 2 were assigned to scope statements or title pages. Recommendation: major revision. The discourse-level findings and the round-1 reliability story stand; the round-2 instrument counts, the Chinese and Asian results, the standards' depth shares, the "not AI" exclusions and the core-principle reversal should not be relied on until the cleaning bug is fixed and the affected documents re-annotated, a round-2 reliability sample (model and human) is reported, and stub captures are removed from the instrument counts.

## 5. The five changes that would most improve validity

1. **Fix the cleaner and re-annotate the non-Latin documents.** Make the short-line rule character-based or skip it for CJK lines, verify that >= 95 per cent of characters survive for every language, re-annotate every zh/ja/ko/th document in both rounds and every document whose stored input is under 90 per cent of its cleaned text, publish `share_seen` per document, and recompute the language, Asian-region and `not_ai` results. Until this is done, withdraw the "second language" statement and the Asian comparisons.
2. **Report reliability for round 2 and add a human gold sample.** Complete the 160-document stratified round-2 sample (second model and re-run) and print it beside Table 26; double-code at least 150 documents by humans stratified by language x class x length x capture type, with Chinese and Japanese readers, on the set-defining fields and the six embodied scores; correct the headline shares and odds ratios for label error (PPI or DSL) and give label-flip intervals from the paired runs.
3. **Demote stub captures from the instrument counts.** Treat landing/catalogue/title pages, agent abstracts and captures below a character floor as inventory records with metadata labels only; cap principle scores at 1 for scope-only standards; add `scope_only`, `meta_how` and `share_seen` to the master table; report every instrument count with and without these records, and apply the v2 D4 rule mechanically to both rounds.
4. **Make set membership coherent and text-grounded.** Enforce consistency constraints at annotation time (not_ai => no categories, relevance <= 1; bill => not binding; soft => not legally binding; mandatory standards => mandated), re-query violations, make the strict set (relevance >= 2) primary, keep "robotics without AI" as its own stratum rather than discarding the Machinery Directive lineage, and for texts over the window use chunking or keyword-anchored retrieval with an explicit `unknown`-if-absent instruction tested by a title-masking control.
5. **Control for collection design and stop calling model-vs-model checks independent.** Add round/source, page quality and log text length to the adjusted models (or stratify), present the round-1-only and substantive-only comparisons next to the pooled ones and reframe the v2-to-v3 "reversals" as collection changes; relabel T57 as model-vs-agent/harvester consistency and split it by source; ship the round-2 `_inputs`, logs and a run manifest (CLI version, model id, policy, hash, cost, purges) so that the audit in this review can be repeated from the package alone.
