## The eight ICLR "must" items

1. **Round as covariate; round-1 and round-2 versions of the genre comparison; abstract and Discussion rewritten; binding-share test and "30 to 60" dropped as findings.** Met. T20 adjusts for class, region, round and year (Section 4, Table 20); T20b gives the within-round contrasts for all documents and for instruments and is the primary result of Section 5.5; T30 adds round-1-only and round-covariate trend models; the abstract and Discussion paragraphs 2 and 3 present the pooled reversal as a composition effect; the binding-share test and the jurisdictions line are stated in Section 5.2 as consequences of the design. Partly open: the Mantel-Haenszel analysis (T34) still stratifies by class only and T28 adjusts for class and region without round; both are labelled as such in the text and T28 in the limitations.
2. **State the construct; report headline counts under both definitions.** Met. Section 3 defines the object as the governance of the machine classes in which embodied AI is deployed; the abstract, Section 5.1 and Table 53b give the counts under the machine-class construct, the robot-relevance tier and the two text-based AI-content tiers (505 / 408 / 395 / 153; 195 / 168 / 140 / 15).
3. **Precision audit; documented rule for ancillary instruments and copies; recompute T53, T22 and 5.1; gap index by source.** Met except the last item. Section 4 and Table 62 report the 100-document audit with three main criteria and intervals; the principal-instrument rule is documented and applied; T22 and Section 5.3 and 5.4 are on the principal unit, T53 on the comparable unit with that stated. The gap index by collection source was not computed and is listed in the limitations.
4. **Round-2 reliability sample with per-field kappa including level-2; unweighted and level-2 tables or delete the sentence.** Met. 160-document stratified round-2 sample (second model and re-run) reported in Sections 4 and 5.8 with the weak fields named; S09b holds the unweighted and level-2 variants for all six comparisons and is cited.
5. **Recompute Table 9 on content-analysed captures; correct the abstract.** Met. T09 is on content-analysed captures; the abstract no longer carries depth percentages; Section 5.5 quotes the principal binding shares and reports the standards' scope-page shares as a separate measurement on 62 captures.

## Not done, and why

- Human validation of labels (ICLR could 15; review log N5): no human coders are available; the S4.5 protocol remains the stated next step, and the paper says "no person read or labelled any document" in the abstract, methods and limitations.
- Within-genre regional divergence (T29b): the pipeline writes these tables only when every region has at least ten documents of the genre; the Middle East and Latin America do not, so no T29b file exists and the paper reports the regional "values" divergence as not separable from genre mix, using T31 (research-only distances) instead.
- Within-round tests for the proximity and liability declines: T30 carries six outcomes and not these two; the paper withdraws the pooled declines as trends rather than asserting a within-round result it cannot cite.
- Gap index by collection source (ICLR must 3, last clause): not computed; stated in the limitations.
- Round covariate in T28 and T34: not added; both tables are described with their actual covariates.
- Version dating of consolidated statutes (must 6): deferred to a third round; reframed as above.
- Research-only trend models (could 17): not run; T28's research-only shares are quoted instead.
- `median_fraction_seen` in T33 (could 16): the table caption still defines it against the first excerpt; the paper quotes T33 only for capture quality.
- Table 9's soft-law, standards and draft rows are content-analysed captures of every legal text of that class rather than principal instruments only, and T09 does not apply the 150-word floor that F23 applies (binding 195 in T09 against 222 in F23); the paper quotes T09 and names its base for each row.
- Figure 6 remains on the all-texts unit by binding nature; the paper says so and uses it only with the dating caveat.
- The pipeline-generated caption of Table 53 still reads "v2 ... versus v3/v4"; the paper's text frames the table as round 1 alone against the merged collection.
- Length: 63 KB against 52 KB for the previous version. The brief asked for about 50 KB; the increase is the required new material (Sections 5.1 and 5.2, the counting-unit and precision-audit methods, round-2 reliability, the expanded limitations). Percentage lists were cut as asked; further cuts would remove content the brief requires.
