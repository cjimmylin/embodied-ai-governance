Who governs the robots? · volunteer team

Volunteer sign-up and tracker

Ten pieces of work that a person can do and the study's AI agents could not, ordered by how much they change the results. Tag yourself (or a teammate) by email on a task, open its instructions, and keep its counter and status up to date so everyone can see where the work stands.

1. Choose a taskRead the cards below; "Needs people" shows where help is short.
2. Tag peopleType one or more email addresses on a card and press Tag. Click a person's role to switch lead and helper.
3. Do the workOpen "What to do" on the card for the steps, the files and what to hand back.
4. Report progressRaise the counter, set the status, and post a one-line update when you stop for the day.
Signs your updates and fills the "Tag me" buttons. It is remembered in this browser only. Emails you tag on a task are visible to everyone who can open this page.
