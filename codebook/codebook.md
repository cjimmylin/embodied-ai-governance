# Codebook: Global Governance of Embodied AI and Robotics — Document Annotation Scheme (v1.0)

You are annotating one document from a multilingual corpus of AI-governance sources collected by region. Read the document excerpt and metadata, then fill every field of the JSON schema. Read non-English text in its original language; do not skip it. Code only what the text and metadata support. When the text is a bot-block page or missing, use the title, URL and filename, and set `page_quality` and `confidence` accordingly.

## A. Bibliographic fields
- `title_clean`: the document's real title in its original language (not the filename). For a web page, the page's own heading. If none, a short descriptive title.
- `title_en`: English translation (identical if already English).
- `language`: ISO 639-1 code of the main body text (en, es, pt, fr, de, it, ru, zh, ja, ko, ar, he, tr, pl, vi, th, id, ms, nl, ...).
- `authors_or_issuer`: research outputs: authors as "Surname, Initials" separated by "; " (max 6, then "et al."). Governance instruments, reports and web pages: the issuing body (ministry, agency, legislature, court, standards body, company, NGO, news outlet).
- `issuer_type`: who produced it. `national_government_executive` (ministry, cabinet, president, prime minister's office), `legislature` (parliament, congress, committee), `regulator_or_agency` (data protection authority, competition authority, safety agency, standards-setting public agency), `court_or_judiciary`, `subnational_government` (state, province, city), `intergovernmental_org` (UN, UNESCO, OECD, ILO, EU institutions, ASEAN, African Union, WHO, ISO-member intergovernmental fora), `standards_body` (ISO, IEC, IEEE SA, ANSI, national standards institute), `industry_company`, `industry_association`, `academic_or_research` (universities, journals, research institutes), `ngo_or_civil_society`, `think_tank`, `news_media`, `professional_body` (medical, legal, engineering associations), `other`, `unknown`. EU institutions count as `intergovernmental_org`.
- `pub_year`, `pub_month`: publication, adoption or enactment date of the document itself (for a law, the enactment date; for a web page about a law, the page's own date if stated, else the law's date). `null` if unknown. `date_basis`: `stated_in_document`, `inferred_from_content` (e.g., latest events or citations mentioned), `unknown`.
- `doi`: DOI if present, else null. `venue`: journal, conference, publisher, or website name.

## B. Jurisdiction
- `jurisdiction_country`: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the issuing jurisdiction (for governance instruments, reports, news) or the first author's affiliation country (for research outputs). Use `EU` for European Union institutions, `INT` for international bodies (UN system, OECD, ISO/IEC, IEEE, ILO, WHO, G7, Council of Europe, African Union, ASEAN), `XX` if unknown.
- `jurisdiction_scope`: `international`, `supranational_regional` (EU, ASEAN, AU, Arab League, CARICOM), `national`, `subnational`, `not_applicable` (e.g., a research paper with no jurisdictional claim).
- `countries_discussed`: alpha-2 codes of countries whose law, policy or practice the document analyses or targets (max 10; empty list if none). Include `EU` if the EU is a subject.

## C. Document genre
- `doc_type` (one value):
  - Binding instruments: `statute_or_act` (primary legislation in force or enacted), `regulation_or_decree` (secondary legislation, executive orders, decrees, ordinances, binding rules by regulators), `international_treaty_or_convention`, `court_decision_or_judicial_notice` (judgments, court practice directions).
  - Proposals: `bill_or_draft_law` (introduced or drafted, not enacted), `consultation_or_white_paper` (green/white papers, requests for comment, draft guidance open for consultation).
  - Soft law and policy: `national_strategy_or_action_plan`, `policy_guideline_or_code_of_practice` (operational guidance issued by a public body or regulator), `ethical_framework_or_principles` (principle statements, charters, ethics guidelines), `technical_standard` (ISO, IEC, IEEE, ANSI, national standards; standards catalogue pages count), `intergovernmental_recommendation_or_resolution` (UNESCO Recommendation, OECD Recommendation, UN resolutions, EU Parliament resolutions, declarations such as Bletchley), `government_report_or_review` (reports, reviews, impact assessments, inquiries by public bodies), `parliamentary_material` (committee reports, hearings, testimony, legislative observatory pages), `procurement_or_internal_policy` (procurement notes, internal use policies, agency AI registers, playbooks).
  - Research outputs: `journal_article`, `conference_paper`, `review_or_survey_article` (systematic reviews, scoping reviews, surveys of literature), `book_or_chapter`, `preprint_or_working_paper`, `thesis_or_dissertation`, `editorial_or_commentary` (editorials, book reviews, short commentary in scholarly venues).
  - Grey literature and communication: `industry_or_ngo_report` (company white papers, consultancy reports, NGO reports, trade-association submissions), `news_article` (journalism), `press_release_or_government_news` (announcements by a government, agency or organisation), `blog_or_opinion` (opinion pieces, blog posts, magazine essays), `web_page_informational` (landing pages, programme descriptions, FAQs, portal pages, project fact sheets, podcasts and event pages), `other`, `unusable` (error page, bot-block page, empty or unreadable).
- `doc_class` (coarse): `governance_instrument_binding` (statute, regulation, treaty, court decision), `governance_instrument_soft` (strategy, guideline, ethical framework, standard, recommendation, procurement policy), `governance_proposal_or_draft` (bill, consultation, white paper), `government_report_or_parliamentary` (reports, reviews, parliamentary material), `research_output`, `grey_literature` (industry/NGO reports), `communication_or_news` (news, press releases, blogs, informational pages), `unusable`.
- `is_governance_document`: true only if the document itself governs or prescribes (law, regulation, standard, guideline, strategy, principles, recommendation, court rule, procurement policy, bill). A news story or paper about such an instrument is false. A government web page that reproduces the instrument's content counts as true.
- `instrument_status` (only when `is_governance_document` is true; otherwise `not_applicable`): `proposal_or_concept` (idea or recommendation not yet drafted as an instrument), `draft_or_bill_under_consideration`, `consultation_open`, `adopted_not_yet_in_force`, `in_force` (binding instruments currently applicable), `published_current` (soft-law instruments and standards that are current), `under_development` (standards in preparation, e.g., ISO/WD, ISO/CD, ISO/PWI), `superseded_or_repealed`, `withdrawn_or_lapsed`, `unknown`.
- `binding_nature`: `legally_binding`, `voluntary_soft_law`, `technical_standard_voluntary`, `technical_standard_mandated` (standard made mandatory by law), `not_applicable`.
- `publication_stage` (research and grey outputs; otherwise `not_applicable`): `peer_reviewed`, `preprint`, `book`, `thesis`, `grey_literature`, `unknown`.

## D. Embodied-AI scope and taxonomy
- `ai_scope`: `general_ai_only` (AI governance with no meaningful robotics, vehicle, drone or other physical-system content), `general_ai_with_embodied_provisions` (a general AI document that contains explicit provisions, sections or examples on robots, autonomous vehicles, drones, medical devices with actuation, or other physical AI), `embodied_ai_robotics_general` (about robots or physical AI as a class), `embodied_ai_specific_category` (about one or a few specific embodied applications), `not_ai` (not about AI or robotics).
- `embodiment_status`: `physical_embodied` (physical robots, vehicles, drones, machines, prostheses), `software_agent_called_robot` (RPA, trading robots, robo-advisors, robot lawyers or judges, chatbots, web crawlers; "robot" is used for a software agent only), `mixed`, `none`.
- `embodied_categories` (multi-label; empty if none). Robotics and physical-AI taxonomy:
  - Industrial and logistics: `industrial_manipulators` (fixed industrial arms, caged robots), `collaborative_robots` (cobots sharing workspace with humans), `mobile_industrial_logistics` (AMRs, AGVs, warehouse and forklift robots, industrial trucks), `delivery_robots` (sidewalk and last-mile delivery robots).
  - Service and social: `professional_service_robots` (cleaning, security, hospitality, retail, inspection, rescue robots), `domestic_household_robots` (vacuum, lawn, home assistants), `social_companion_robots` (companionship, emotional interaction, pets), `educational_robots`, `care_assistive_robots` (elder care, disability support, nursing assistance), `toys_entertainment_robots`, `sex_robots`.
  - Medical and body-integrated: `surgical_medical_robots`, `rehabilitation_exoskeleton_prosthetic` (exoskeletons, prostheses, wearable robots, brain-computer-interface-driven actuators), `biohybrid_or_cyborg`.
  - General-purpose: `humanoid_general_purpose` (humanoids, androids, general-purpose embodied agents, embodied foundation models).
  - Vehicles and field: `autonomous_road_vehicles` (cars, trucks, buses, shuttles), `drones_uas` (aerial), `autonomous_maritime_or_rail`, `agricultural_robots`, `space_or_extreme_environment` (space, underwater, nuclear, mining).
  - Security: `military_autonomous_weapons` (LAWS, armed drones, military robots), `security_policing_robots` (police, border, surveillance robots).
  - `other_embodied`.
- `primary_embodied_category`: the single most central category, or `none`.
- `application_sectors` (multi-label): `healthcare`, `elder_care_social_care`, `manufacturing`, `logistics_transport`, `agriculture_food`, `defense_security`, `public_administration_justice`, `education`, `finance`, `retail_hospitality`, `construction`, `energy_environment`, `domestic_consumer`, `research_science`, `cross_sector`, `other`.

## E. Governance content
- `governance_topics` (multi-label, what the document substantively addresses): `ethics_principles`, `civil_liability`, `criminal_liability`, `legal_personhood_robot_rights`, `safety_physical_harm`, `cybersecurity`, `privacy_data_protection`, `human_oversight_control`, `transparency_explainability`, `fairness_bias_discrimination`, `labor_employment`, `human_dignity_autonomy`, `human_robot_interaction_social_effects`, `military_use_ihl`, `certification_conformity_assessment`, `standards_interoperability`, `risk_management_risk_tiers`, `intellectual_property`, `consumer_protection`, `industrial_policy_innovation`, `research_ethics`, `sustainability_environment`, `children_vulnerable_groups`, `trust_acceptance`, `education_training`, `taxation_economic_policy`, `other`. `primary_topic`: the single most central one.
- `principles`: for each principle give 0 (absent), 1 (mentioned in passing), 2 (substantively treated, operationalised or required). Core principles follow Jobin et al. (2019) and the OECD AI Principles; the last six are embodied-specific extensions.
  - `transparency` (transparency, explainability, disclosure), `justice_fairness` (fairness, non-discrimination, equity, access), `non_maleficence` (avoiding harm, safety and security in general, misuse prevention), `responsibility_accountability` (accountability, responsibility, legal liability, redress), `privacy` (privacy, data protection), `beneficence` (benefit, well-being, social good, prosperity), `freedom_autonomy` (human autonomy, consent, freedom from manipulation), `trust` (trustworthiness, public trust), `sustainability` (environmental and economic sustainability), `dignity` (human dignity), `solidarity` (social cohesion, inclusion, solidarity, redistribution), `human_oversight` (human control, human-in-the-loop, meaningful human control), `robustness_security` (technical robustness, reliability, cybersecurity), `inclusive_growth` (inclusive growth, innovation promotion, competitiveness).
  - Embodied-specific: `physical_safety` (bodily harm, collision, force limits, machine safety), `human_robot_proximity` (shared workspace, physical interaction, social or emotional interaction norms), `liability_for_physical_damage` (liability specifically for damage caused by physical systems or products), `autonomy_level_control` (levels of autonomy, degree of automation, human control of actuation), `conformity_assessment_certification` (certification, CE marking, testing, type approval), `dual_use_military` (weaponisation, dual use).
- `governance_mechanisms` (multi-label; mechanisms the document establishes, requires or recommends): `risk_based_tiering`, `prohibitions_bans`, `licensing_registration`, `conformity_assessment_certification`, `technical_standards_reference`, `liability_regime`, `insurance_requirement`, `human_oversight_requirement`, `transparency_disclosure_labelling`, `data_governance_requirements`, `testing_sandboxes`, `incident_reporting`, `audits_impact_assessment`, `oversight_body_creation`, `funding_or_incentives`, `education_training_programs`, `international_cooperation`, `self_regulation_codes`, `none`.
- `named_instruments_referenced`: canonical names of existing instruments the document cites or relies on, from this list where applicable, else a short canonical name: "EU AI Act", "EU Machinery Regulation", "EU Product Liability Directive", "EU AI Liability Directive (proposal)", "GDPR", "EU Civil Law Rules on Robotics 2017", "UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI", "OECD AI Principles", "Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI", "NIST AI RMF", "ISO 10218", "ISO 13482", "ISO 3691-4", "ISO/TS 15066", "ISO/IEC 42001", "ISO/IEC 23894", "ISO/IEC 22989", "IEEE 7000 series", "IEEE P7001", "ANSI/A3 R15.06", "Asimov's Laws of Robotics", "Korea Intelligent Robots Act", "Korea AI Basic Act", "Japan AI Promotion Act", "China Generative AI Measures", "US Executive Order 14110", "Colorado AI Act", "Bletchley Declaration", "Hiroshima Process", "UN LAWS/CCW GGE". Empty list if none.
- `robot_legal_personhood_position`: `supports`, `opposes`, `discusses_neutral`, `not_discussed`.
- `risk_framing`: dominant framing: `risk_based`, `rights_based`, `principle_or_ethics_based`, `innovation_promotion`, `liability_or_accountability_based`, `mixed`, `not_applicable`.
- `stance_toward_regulation` (for research, commentary, reports): `calls_for_new_regulation`, `existing_law_sufficient_or_adapt`, `self_regulation_or_standards_preferred`, `descriptive_or_neutral`, `not_applicable`.
- `methodology` (research outputs only): `conceptual_or_normative`, `legal_doctrinal`, `empirical_qualitative`, `empirical_quantitative`, `technical_or_engineering`, `systematic_or_scoping_review`, `case_study`, `mixed`, `not_applicable`.
- `faith_or_religious_perspective`: true if the document argues from a religious or theological standpoint (e.g., Islamic jurisprudence, Christian anthropology, Confucian ethics presented as religious tradition).

## F. Quality and summary
- `page_quality`: `substantive_document` (full document or article), `partial_or_excerpt` (abstract only, truncated, cover page with summary), `landing_or_index_page` (navigation, catalogue, search or portal page with little substantive content), `error_or_blocked_page` (bot wall, 403, "request unsuccessful"), `non_text` (scanned or image-only).
- `relevance_to_embodied_ai_governance`: 0 (irrelevant), 1 (tangential: general AI or passing mention), 2 (relevant: governance of robots or physical AI is a substantial theme), 3 (central: the document is primarily about governing robots or physical AI).
- `summary_en`: at most 60 words, in English, stating what the document is and its main governance point.
- `key_provisions_or_claims`: up to 5 short English statements of the most important provisions (instruments) or claims and recommendations (papers, reports).
- `confidence`: your overall confidence in the annotation from 0.0 to 1.0 (use <= 0.4 when text is missing or blocked).

## Decision rules
1. Genre before content: decide `doc_type` from the document's own form, not from its topic. A news article about a law is `news_article`, not `statute_or_act`.
2. A government web page that explains a strategy is `web_page_informational` unless it reproduces the strategy text, in which case use the strategy's type.
3. Standards catalogue pages (ISO/IEC/IEEE store or committee pages) are `technical_standard` with `page_quality` = `landing_or_index_page` if only metadata is shown.
4. Code `embodied_categories` from substantive discussion, not from one incidental word. A general AI act that defines robots as AI systems but has no robot-specific provisions is `general_ai_only`.
5. Trading robots, RPA bots, robo-advisors, robot lawyers, chatbots and web crawlers are `software_agent_called_robot` with `embodied_categories` empty, unless physical robots are also treated.
6. Principles: give 2 only when the principle is a required element, a dedicated section, or a central argument.
7. For `jurisdiction_country` of research outputs, use the first author's affiliation if it can be found in the text; otherwise the country whose law the paper analyses; otherwise `XX`.
8. Outlets such as The Conversation, newspapers, magazines and broadcasters are `news_media` even when the author is an academic; the genre is then `news_article` or `blog_or_opinion`.
9. For research outputs, `jurisdiction_scope` is the scope of the jurisdiction whose law or policy the paper analyses (`national` for a paper on Korean law, `supranational_regional` for a paper on the EU AI Act); use `not_applicable` only when no specific jurisdiction is analysed.
10. Do not invent dates. Prefer an explicit date in the text; otherwise infer from the latest dated event or citation and set `date_basis` accordingly.
