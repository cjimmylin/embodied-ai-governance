# Round-2 collector reports (condensed)

Nine collector agents and one text-acquisition agent ran in parallel with explicit briefs, the candidate schema (CANDIDATE_SCHEMA.md) and the list of round-1 holdings to exclude. Every agent reported that the shared web-search budget was exhausted part-way through its brief, after which it worked only from official databases, APIs and direct fetches of known official sites. The lists of items each agent looked for but could not find or verify are reproduced here because they bound what round 2 can claim.
